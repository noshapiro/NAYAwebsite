## Block 2 — Digital Twins

| Element | Copy |
|--------|------|
| Index | 02 |
| H3 | Digital twins that behave like the real person |
| Tagline | Not the voice. The whole person. |
| Description | Anyone can clone a voice or upload some notes. We replicate everything that makes a person *them* — their habits, the way they make decisions, their judgment, their tone, the way they show up. A real personality you can deploy at scale. |

*Italic on **them** is intentional.*

**How it works**

1. **Voice + face cloning** — High-fidelity likeness in audio and avatar. The surface layer most "twins" stop at.
2. **NearuVibe™ behavioral layer** — Not just emotion detection. The twin reads the other person's state in real time — and behaves the way the real human would in that exact moment. Empathy where they'd be empathetic, directness where they'd be direct.
3. **Personality Matrix** — Captures methodology, decision patterns, tone, beliefs, conversational style. The twin is constrained to the real person's way of thinking — not the LLM's generic baseline.
4. **Tacit knowledge capture** — Structured interviews, shadow-mode learning, and adversarial probing extract the intuitive expertise the person has never written down.
5. **Persistent memory across sessions** — Every conversation builds relationship history. The twin remembers context, emotional state, and what mattered last time.

**Result callout**

**The result:** a twin that maintains the real human's relationships, methodology, and presence at 10× their natural capacity. Usable for sleep consultants, lactation experts, coaches, therapists' adjuncts, educators, executives.
